Subscriber.dev sends the emails your product sends automatically, like a welcome email when someone signs up, a reminder before their trial ends, or an announcement to everyone. They go out through your own email provider. There's no dashboard: you set it up and run it through your AI agent over MCP, the CLI, or the REST API.

## How it fits together

There are four things to know about:

- **Workspaces** hold your team's billing and environments. Every workspace starts with a **Production** environment, and you can add more, such as staging.
- **[Subscribers](/subscribers)** are the people you email. They belong to an environment and are matched by `email`, `externalId` (your own ID) or their `publicId`.
- **Integrations** connect an environment to an email provider (any SMTP server, or Cloudflare Email) and to event sources such as Stripe. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview).
- **Workflows** are a list of email or webhook steps with delays. Your app, your agent or a Stripe event triggers them for one subscriber, or you send them to everyone as a broadcast.
